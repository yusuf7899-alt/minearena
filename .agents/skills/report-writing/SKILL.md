# Pre-Audit Report Writing Skill

## Purpose
This skill governs the composition, structuring, and formatting of the official **Pre-Audit Working Paper Report** generated by Moneta. The output must meet professional standards expected by audit partners and regulatory bodies.

## Required Report Sections

### 1. Executive Pre-Audit Memorandum
- **Engagement Details:** Entity Name, Ticker, CIK, Form (10-K / 10-K/A), Fiscal Year End, Filing Acceptance Timestamp, Review Date, Reviewer Name & Title.
- **Pre-Audit Opinion Summary:** Statement of overall consistency (Clean Tie-Out / Exceptions Noted / Material Misstatements Detected).
- **Core Statistics:** Total Metrics Evaluated, Extraction Accuracy vs XBRL Ground Truth, Auto-Approved Count, Escalated Count, Escalation Precision.

### 2. Primary Financial Statements & Reconciliation Table
A comprehensive comparative ledger containing:
- Metric Concept (Line item description).
- Extracted Value (with period end and detected scale).
- XBRL Ground Truth Value (with exact US-GAAP tag).
- Variance (Dollar delta and % swing).
- Final Disposition (Auto-Approved / Auditor Verified / Edited / Rejected).
- Supporting Evidence (Filing page, note, or table reference).

### 3. Four-Factor Linkage & Accounting Equation Tie-Outs
- **Balance Sheet Identity:** Detailed schedule of Total Assets vs (Total Liabilities + Stockholders' Equity). Explicitly identifies whether Liabilities were tagged or derived.
- **P&L to Balance Sheet Bridge:** Step-by-step Retained Earnings Roll-Forward:
  $$\text{Ending RE} = \text{Beginning RE} + \text{Net Income} - \text{Dividends} \pm \text{Reconciling Disclosures}$$
- **Cash Flow Reconciliation:** Ending Cash tie-out and Operating Cash Flow sanity comparison.
- **Segment Breakdown:** Disclosed segment totals vs consolidated revenues/profits.

### 4. Escalated Exceptions & Auditor Q&A Highlights
- Complete log of all flagged metrics.
- Auditor investigation dialogue: queries posed, read-only tools invoked, cited footnotes returned.
- Final human action taken: original value, corrected value (if edited), justification note, reviewer ID, timestamp.

### 5. Append-Only Audit Trail
- Chronological, tamper-evident transaction log of every state change and human action.
- Unique session identifier and cryptographic SHA-256 state hash.

### 6. Scope Limitations & Disclaimers
- Standard PCAOB / AICPA pre-audit disclaimer stating that Moneta is an automated analytical pre-audit assistance tool and does not constitute a statutory audit opinion.
